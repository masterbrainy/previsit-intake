Meet PATH

A voice assistant that takes a patient's history at home before the visit, and hands the doctor a history sheet where every line links back to the patient's own words. Try it from both sides.

    Be the patient

Try a red flag

During the conversation, say something like I've had a fever and my neck is really stiff. PATH stops the interview, gives fixed emergency instructions, and alerts the hospital straight away.

Everyone in this demo is fictional, and nothing is sent by text or email. Please don't enter real health information. Use the switcher at the bottom of any page to change views.
